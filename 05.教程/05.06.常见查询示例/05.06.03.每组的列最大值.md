### 5.6.3 每组的列最大值

**任务：找出每种商品的最高价？**

```sql
SELECT article, MAX(price) AS price
FROM   shop
GROUP BY article
ORDER BY article;

+---------+-------+
| article | price |
+---------+-------+
|    0001 |  3.99 |
|    0002 | 10.99 |
|    0003 |  1.69 |
|    0004 | 19.95 |
+---------+-------+
```
